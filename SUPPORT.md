# Getting help

Everything on GitHub is public. Never post personal data in an issue, a pull request or a
discussion: no email addresses, invoices, tokens, links to things you've shared, or screenshots
that show your data.

## Your account on the hosted service

For anything about your account at work.cronumstudio.com (signing in, your plan or payments,
exporting or deleting your data, something you can no longer reach), email
**hello@cronumstudio.com** from the address on your account. Please don't open a public issue for
these.

## Running Cronum Work yourself

Questions about self-hosting Cronum Work (installing, configuring, upgrading) go to the
[issues of cronumstudio/work](https://github.com/cronumstudio/work/issues). Search first: it may
already have an answer. Say which version you run and how (Docker or Node, behind which proxy),
and include the relevant log lines with secrets, domains and email addresses removed.

## Bugs and ideas

Open an issue in the repository it is about. [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/cronumstudio/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
says which one.

## Security problems

Never in public: see the [security policy](https://github.com/cronumstudio/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

Support is best effort: Cronum Studio is maintained by one person.
