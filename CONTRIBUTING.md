# Contributing to Cronum Studio

Thank you for helping. These repositories are Cronum Studio's apps and the platform they share
(suite-core), published under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. Cronum Studio also runs
them as a hosted service.

## Issues

Bugs, questions and ideas are welcome as issues in the repository they are about. For a bug, say
what you did, what you expected and what happened, and which version (`/version` of the app, or
the release) you were on. Report security problems privately, never in an issue: see the
repository's security policy, or write to the address on cronumstudio.com.

## Pull requests

Before a pull request can be merged, its author signs the
[Contributor License Agreement](https://github.com/cronumstudio/.github/blob/main/CLA.md) once.
On your first pull request, a bot asks you to sign it with a comment. You keep the copyright of
your work. The agreement lets Cronum Studio keep the projects open and also offer them as a
hosted service. One signature covers every repository of the organization.

A good pull request:

- does one thing, and says why in its description;
- keeps the repository's style (CONVENTIONS.md or CLAUDE.md, where there is one);
- comes with tests, and `npm test` passes;
- writes code, comments, commits and documentation in English.

For anything large, open an issue first, so the design can be agreed before the work.
