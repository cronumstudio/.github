# Cronum Studio Corporate Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.1, 7 December 2026

This agreement is for a company or other organization whose employees or contractors contribute
to Cronum Studio projects, when the organization has rights in what they contribute, for example
as their employer. For those contributions it gives Cronum Studio the same licenses as the
individual agreement, version 1.1
([cla/CLA-1.1.md](https://github.com/cronumstudio/.github/blob/main/cla/CLA-1.1.md), "the
individual CLA"). The people who contribute still sign the individual CLA on their pull requests,
as the bot asks: this agreement covers the organization's rights, and the individual CLA covers
each person's own, including their rights as authors.

To sign it, fill in the details at the end and Schedule A, have it signed by someone who can
legally bind the organization, and send it to hello@cronumstudio.com. Cronum Studio signs it too
and sends a copy back. It applies from the date of the last signature.

trading as Cronum Studio, and whoever takes his place under section 6 or by inheritance.

listed in Schedule B, if the person who signs can also bind them.

accounts, as the Company updates it under section 4.

translations, images and designs, except what is marked in writing as "Not a Contribution" when
it is submitted. Earlier submissions are covered too if Schedule A lists them.

## 6. General

**Versions.** This is version 1.1, numbered like the individual CLA it goes with. Each version is
kept unchanged in the `cla` folder of github.com/cronumstudio/.github. A new version applies only
to Contributions submitted after the Company signs it.

**Ending it.** The Company can end this agreement for future Contributions at any time by writing
to hello@cronumstudio.com. It keeps applying to everything submitted before.

**Transfer.** Cronum Studio may transfer this agreement, with its rights and obligations, to
whoever takes over the projects, for example a company Martin Andersen sets up. If someone takes
over only some of the projects, Cronum Studio may transfer this agreement to them only for the
Contributions to those projects. The Company agrees to these transfers now, and they don't need
its consent again. From then on, "Cronum Studio" means the new owner for the Contributions
transferred, and it takes on the obligations of this agreement for them. Cronum Studio will
announce any transfer in github.com/cronumstudio/.github. Licenses already granted to others are
not affected.

**Notices.** Notices under this agreement go by email: to hello@cronumstudio.com, and to the
Company's contact person named below.

**Personal data.** Cronum Studio (Martin Andersen) uses the names, positions and contact details
in this agreement, and the GitHub accounts in Schedule A, only to manage this agreement and the
Contributions it covers, on its legitimate interest in doing so and in being able to show where
its code comes from. It keeps the signed copy privately for as long as it keeps the record of
those Contributions. The people named can exercise their data protection rights by writing to
hello@cronumstudio.com, and can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD); the privacy
policy at https://cronumstudio.com/privacy/#github explains the rest. The Company passes this
information on to them.

**Law and courts.** This agreement is governed by Spanish law. The courts of A Coruña (Spain) have
exclusive jurisdiction, where the law allows that choice.

**Signing.** This agreement can be signed electronically, or on paper and scanned. Each party
keeps a signed copy.

**Language.** This agreement is in English. A translation, if one is made, is only for
information.

If a court finds part of this agreement invalid, the rest still applies.
