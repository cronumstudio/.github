# Cronum Studio trademark policy

The code of Cronum Work, its modules and suite-core is free software under the GNU Affero
General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0-only). That licence covers the code. It gives no rights to
Cronum Studio's names and brand, which this policy covers. Nothing here limits what the AGPL lets
you do with the code itself.

## The marks

- The names **Cronum**, **Cronum Work** and **Cronum Studio**, and the names of the modules when
  they are presented as Cronum products.
- The Cronum logos and app icons.
- The ring mark.
- The Cronum mascot.

The logo and icon files are in the repositories so the apps can run. Being there doesn't license
them as marks.

## What you can do without asking

- **Run unmodified copies, including self-hosting**, for yourself or your organization, with
  the original names and icons. Setting configuration options, and installing the official
  updates, doesn't count as modifying.
- **Say truthfully what your work is based on**: "based on Cronum Work", "a fork of Cronum
  Work", "works with Cronum Work".
- **Refer to Cronum Studio and its products by name** in articles, reviews, tutorials, talks
  and comparisons, as long as it's clear you aren't Cronum Studio.
- **Keep the credits**: the "by Cronum Studio" signature, and the copyright and licence notices,
  which the AGPL asks you to keep anyway.

## What needs another name

If you make a modified version available to others (a fork, a rebranded build, a container
image, or a hosted service that runs modified code), you must:

- give it a name that isn't "Cronum", doesn't contain it and can't be confused with it, and use
  that name for its domain too;
- replace the Cronum logos, app icons, ring mark and mascot with your own;
- not suggest that Cronum Studio made, endorses or supports it.

You are welcome to say that it is based on Cronum Work.

If you offer an unmodified copy as a service to people outside your organization, the app may
keep showing its own name and icons, but the service you offer needs its own name and domain,
and must say that it isn't run by Cronum Studio.

## Examples

Fine:

- "Acme Planner, based on Cronum Work"
- "A fork of Cronum Work with a calendar module"
- "We self-host Cronum Work for our team"

Not fine without permission:

- "Cronum Work Pro", "Cronum for Teams", "Acme Cronum"
- a domain such as cronum-hosting.example
- a modified build that still shows the Cronum logos or icons

## Asking for permission

For anything this policy doesn't cover, or if you're unsure, write to
hello@cronumstudio.com. We're happy to help a project say clearly where it comes from.
